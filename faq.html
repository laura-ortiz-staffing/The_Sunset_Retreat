Frequently Asked Questions

How many guests can stay?

Up to 16 guests. Up to 20 is possible with couch sleeping, subject to approval and additional fees.

Is the pond safe for kids?

The private pond has a beach area and is about 6 ft deep at its max. It's unheated, unfiltered, and unsupervised — life jackets are recommended and diving isn't allowed. Pets are not permitted in the pond.

Can we bring pets?

Up to 2 approved pets, non-shedding/hypoallergenic breeds only, with a fee. Pets aren't allowed in the pond.

Is the house accessible?

Not fully ADA accessible — there are stairs to the upper bedrooms and basement. One primary bedroom and bath are on the first floor.

Can we host a wedding or party?

No — Sunset View Retreat is family-focused and does not host weddings, parties, or events.

What's provided in the kitchen?

A fully equipped kitchen (fridge, freezer, stove, oven, microwave, dishwasher, cookware, dishes), plus an espresso machine, drip coffee maker, and Keurig. Starter household supplies, linens, and towels are provided.

Where do we park?

Parking for about 10 vehicles. Please don't park on the lawn or road. Porch outlets are available for small EV chargers.

What are check-in and checkout times?

Check-in is 4:00 PM, checkout is 11:00 AM. Entry is self check-in via keypad; directions are sent before arrival since GPS may not find the driveway.

Do you offer a discount for returning guests?

Yes — book direct again within 6 months of your stay and get 10% off.
